Meet the artist

Hi, I’m Anastasia.

I’m an artist with a background in design and more than a decade spent working in the visual world of 3D.

These days, I’m drawn to much simpler things: food on the table, hands around a cup, familiar objects, and small scenes from everyday life. I paint them digitally and with traditional materials, giving ordinary things a little more attention than they usually get.

Art has been part of my life since childhood, and now I’m building a practice of my own — one piece at a time.

A favourite meal, a place worth remembering, an object with a story — or a broader visual idea for a space, collection or project.

I take on selected private and commercial commissions, from individual artworks to larger visual projects. Each piece is developed in my own visual language and shaped around its subject, setting and purpose.

What to include in your enquiry +

Tell me a little about your idea, what the artwork is for, and any preferred size, format or timing. References and an approximate budget are welcome if you have them. You don’t need a finished brief to start the conversation.
